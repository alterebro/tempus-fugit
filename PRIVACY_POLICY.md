# Privacy Policy for Tempus Fugit

Effective date: 8 September 2026

Tempus Fugit is designed to work entirely on your device.

## Information processed locally

To calculate and display your estimated remaining time, Tempus Fugit processes the information you choose to enter:

- Birth date
- Country
- Optional gender selection
- Visual theme preference

This information is stored only in your browser’s local storage on your device. The extension performs its calculations locally.

## No collection, transmission, or sharing

Tempus Fugit does not collect, transmit, upload, sell, rent, or share your personal information.

We do not operate servers that receive your extension data. We do not use accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, telemetry, or third-party data processors.

The extension does not access your browsing history, website content, passwords, contacts, or any other browser data.

## Your control over local data

Your settings remain in your browser until you change them, clear the extension’s local data, or uninstall the extension. Because this information never leaves your device, we cannot access, retrieve, or restore it for you.

## Changes to this policy

If Tempus Fugit’s data practices change, this privacy policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
